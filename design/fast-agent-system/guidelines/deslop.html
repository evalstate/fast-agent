✗ KPI capsules / stat pills
Use a number in a sentence or a table row. A stat chip is only OK when that number is the page.
✗ Gradient backgrounds, glass, blur
Flat ivory or petrol. At most one flat tint band per chart.
✗ Soft drop shadows
Keylines do the structure. The only shadow is a solid offset on a sticker or CTA.
✗ Left-border accent cards
Use a full 2px keyline, or nothing.
✗ Emoji and ✨ "AI" sparkles
The atomic sparkle is decoration, never an AI signifier.
✗ Icons stacked above every heading
Words first. Icons only where they do a job.
✗ Tilted data or copy
Only stickers tilt. Numbers stay level.
✗ More than one burst per view
One salesman per room.
✗ Unsourced numbers
Every score sits next to its n, date and disclaimer link.
✗ "Supercharge / unleash / seamless"
Short declaratives, and receipts.
